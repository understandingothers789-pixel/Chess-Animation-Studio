Replaces the knight-based logo with a distinct cyan frame, coral play mark and cream spark on a dark blue tile. Updates the editor brand, favicon and running desktop-window icon. No chess piece or horse silhouette is used in the new mark.

Adds undoable Reset buttons to effect sections: snow, board atmosphere, beat pulses, composition, opener, movement, timing, capture, camera, highlights, badges and keyframes, plus music/audio settings. Snow, board-effect and move-motion numeric controls also have individual reset buttons. Move resets apply to the selection and use each move’s preset defaults.

Keeps the existing portable layout and unchanged official Electron runtime. Extract all files and open Chess Animation Studio.exe. The application remains unsigned; Windows policy approval is not guaranteed.
