# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.41

- Select a move and choose **Add animation or effect → 3D piece flip** to somersault with sculpted chess pieces. Any piece can flip. The **Sculpted 3D** artwork applies to the whole board, so pieces keep their appearance as they move and settle. Choose artwork under **Board & pieces**.
- The **Move** inspector has separate sections for **Main effect**, **Piece size**, and **After effect**. Set the size to stay constant, grow or shrink smoothly, or follow custom scale keys. Movement presets can be applied independently of the size settings.
- Choose **Piece rebound**, **Piece size pulse**, or **Camera push-in** for a follow-up with its own delay, duration, and strength. The clip extends when it needs more room for the follow-up. Timing locks keep its start and end fixed.
- **Move frames** adds thumbnails for the after-effect peak and end. The timeline marks the start, peak, and end of the follow-up. Size and after effect are separate categories for copying settings, saving presets, and applying them to other moves.
