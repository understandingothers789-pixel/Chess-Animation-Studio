# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.30 — Chess Storyboard Studio

Adds a real edited-sequence workspace alongside Full Game. Build a storyboard from selected, potentially nonconsecutive source moves, legal position holds, and title cards. Drag or keyboard-reorder shots, duplicate, trim, retime, edit local captions/arrows/circles, and override camera or animation without rearranging the source game. Missing source identities remain visible for repair.

Discover moments using legal piece, capture, check, mate, promotion, and castling filters, existing analysis results, and special animation settings. Preview five editable narrative templates before applying one undoable operation. No tactical evaluations or moves are invented.

Preview, presentation, PNG capture, and MP4 use one deterministic output-to-source mapping. Soundtrack music continues on output time; move/capture/knight cues map into selected shot occurrences and stop at cuts. Source and storyboard beat domains are explicit. Export supports all four formats, Shorts framing, 24/30/60 FPS, cancellation, and consistent even dimensions up to 4096 pixels.

Uses an idle thumbnail queue borrowing the preview renderer, a bounded 48-image cache, and a four-entry legal-source context cache. Direct cuts are supported; crossfades and audio time stretching are deferred. Source cue envelopes retain their natural pitch and length, clipped to shot boundaries.
