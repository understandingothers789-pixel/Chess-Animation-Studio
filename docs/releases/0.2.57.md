# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.57 for Windows x64

- **Puzzle Challenge** adds a timed countdown and reveals the selected move.
- **Tactical Breakdown** adds editable arrows and rings for move routes, checks, and castling.
- **Slow-motion replay** adds a separate editable shot at 0.35×, 0.5×, or 0.75× of the selected shot’s speed.
- **Story templates** shows rendered shot thumbnails before applying a template.

Extract the portable ZIP and open **Chess Animation Studio.exe**.
