# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.51

- Music analysis listens to both channels and measures changes in bass, midrange, and treble against the surrounding music. The waveform shows sample peaks and average energy, and strong hits are marked in gold.
- Choose **Prefer strong hits** in **Land moves on beats** for batch timing, or **Strongest nearby hit** for the active move. **Move frames → Effect timing** can place the first beat-zoom pulse on a strong hit. Storyboard can copy strong soundtrack hits to its output beat track and align a shot ending to one.
- Reanalysis keeps corrected markers, deliberate deletions, and existing timing assignments. Markers outside the soundtrack trim stay saved and are skipped for playback and alignment.
