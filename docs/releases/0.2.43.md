# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.43

Thin-edge spin, Capture flip, Dramatic landing, and custom X- or Y-axis turns now show deeper side walls with Nibbler, Neo, and Lichess artwork. The face retains its artwork and size profile. Sculpted 3D pieces continue to show their physical profiles throughout the turn.

**Board & pieces → Solid sides on turns** is enabled by default for illustrated artwork. Turn it off to use the earlier, thinner profile.
