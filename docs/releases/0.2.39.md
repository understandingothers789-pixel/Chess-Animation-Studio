New Thin-edge spin, Piece flip, and Capture flip presets keep the piece at its normal size. Existing unkeyed turns with automatic sizing ease back to normal by landing. Under **Move → Piece size**, choose **Keep normal size** or **Grow and return smoothly**. Adjust **Peak piece size** and **Size return span** to control how large the piece grows and how early it begins easing down. **Original size curve** is available for matching earlier renders. Custom scale keys still take priority. Use **Use size profile** to remove those keys from the selected moves.

Open **Move frames** beneath the board to see rendered departure, mid-move, landing, and settled frames for the selected move. Click a frame to seek, scrub within the move, or step by the export frame rate. The strip shows the auditioned preset during preset comparison and refreshes after you adjust the move. Pause playback to generate new thumbnails.
