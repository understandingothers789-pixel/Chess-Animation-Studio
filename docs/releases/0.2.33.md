# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.33

Select any visible text layer directly in the preview, drag it, resize wrapping width, nudge with arrow keys, and manage its wording, styling, timing, duplication and stacking in the synchronized shot inspector. Selection geometry comes from rendered text. Alt bypasses snapping; Shift nudges farther. Inherited source and outgoing-transition text explain their ownership instead of modifying another shot.

Incoming fade-through-black and cross-dissolve transitions blend completed scenes in linear light. They occupy the start of the destination shot, freeze the previous shot's final state, preserve output duration and audio timing, and evaluate independently of playback history. Cut remains the default. Transition controls include duration, easing and preview.
