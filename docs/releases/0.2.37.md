Preset previews now stay with the selected move. Loop a move, compare with the original, and apply movement without changing timing, camera, effects, or custom keys. An explicit option applies the full preset.

Piece flip has quick controls for direction, hop height, and turn speed. Set a landing beat to retime the move and check the result on the timeline. Beat spotlight has editable ease times and can lead into the landing.

The keyframe editor shows the preset curve and can key the current value at the playhead. Saved presets can be updated, downloaded as JSON, and imported into another project.
