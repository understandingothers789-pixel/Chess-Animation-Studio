# Chess Animation Studio 0.2.53

- Open **Inspect move frames** from the move inspector to see departure, motion, landing, and settled frames under the board. Previous and next frame controls jump into the selected move when the playhead is outside it.
- The animation library shows recent presets in the order you applied them, gives favorites a filled star, and offers a way back when search or filters find no presets.
- Includes improved flip and landing presets, Stockfish-verified sacrifice badges, faster frame evaluation, and more reliable large audio mixes.

Extract the ZIP and open `Chess Animation Studio.exe`. The executable is unsigned.
